The export of engineering goods plays a crucial role in the global economy by enabling international exchange of technological advancements and industrial expertise. These goods include heavy machinery, transportation equipment, specialized tools, and electronic components. Such exports stimulate economic growth, generate employment, and contribute to sharing advanced technical skills worldwide.

Exporting engineering goods involves intricate supply chains. Elements like trade agreements, tariffs, and transportation infrastructure significantly influence competitiveness. Engineering exports aid the development of emerging economies and strengthen global cooperation in sustainable technologies and infrastructure development.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Product Sourcing?
Product sourcing is the process of finding trustworthy manufacturers or suppliers who can produce or deliver quality products that fit your business needs.
What does a sourcing company do?
A sourcing company identifies reliable manufacturers, develops samples, negotiates prices and terms, supervises production quality, and manages global shipping.
How do you identify qualified suppliers?
We use our extensive database of manufacturers to match your exact requirements. Each potential supplier is evaluated for capability, quality, and reliability before we negotiate optimal pricing and terms.
What quality checks are involved?
We have third-party inspection services. We are also open to customer third-party inspection at any point of the process — from incoming materials to final dispatch.
Do I need to order a full container?
No. Depending on your product and order volume, you can choose part-load, mixed-load, or full-container shipping options.
Is there a minimum order quantity?
Yes, most products have an MOQ, but the quantity varies by item. Contact us with your specific requirements for details.
Do shipments always arrive on schedule?
We aim for timely deliveries, but unexpected factors such as vessel delays, weather, or customs procedures may occasionally impact shipping timelines.
What shipping terms do you follow?
We work with your preferred shipping terms, including Free On Board (FOB), Cost and Freight (CFR), and Cost, Insurance & Freight (CIF).
What Brass and SS material grades does Alije Group use?
IS 319 Grade I/II, BS 2874 CZ121/CZ122, EN 12164 CW614N/CW617N, ASTM B16 C36000, SS 303, SS 304, SS 316.
Do you provide customization or OEM production?
Yes. We specialise in OEM and custom manufacturing based on customer drawings, samples, or CAD models with private labelling and export packaging.
Do you export internationally?
Yes, Alije Group exports to USA, Canada, Australia, UK, France, Austria, Switzerland, Spain, Netherlands and worldwide.
What surface finishes are available?
Natural brass, nickel, chrome, tin, zinc, silver and gold finishes — each polished to a mirror surface with no burrs or defects.
